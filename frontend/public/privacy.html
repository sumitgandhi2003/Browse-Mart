📋 By using BrowseMart, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

📋1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, make a purchase, or contact us:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, address, and shipping details
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history, order details, and payment information
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, products viewed, wishlist and cart interactions
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and operating system

⚙️2. How We Use Your Information

BrowseMart uses the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process orders and send order confirmations
  • To personalize your shopping experience
  • To communicate updates, promotions, and support responses
  • To improve the platform's features and performance
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • To comply with legal obligations

🤝3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Sellers: Your shipping address is shared with the seller to fulfill your order
  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties that help operate our platform (hosting, email, payments)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • Business Transfers: In case of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

🔐4. Google OAuth

BrowseMart offers "Sign in with Google" for your convenience. When you use this feature:

  • We receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google
  • We do not receive your Google password
  • We only request the minimum permissions needed (profile + email)
  • You can revoke BrowseMart's access anytime from your Google Account settings
🔒 Google OAuth data is used solely for account creation and authentication. We do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties.

🍪5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in across browser sessions
  • Remember your cart and preferences
  • Analyze platform usage to improve performance

You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings.

🛡️6. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement the following measures:

  • All passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage
  • Authentication uses industry-standard JWT tokens
  • All data transmissions are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Product images are stored securely via Cloudinary
  • Database access is restricted and monitored

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

⚖️7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information via your profile
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Opt out of marketing communications at any time
